Lowest miles finder

One-way saver awards from Singapore or nearby hubs. Award availability is not checked.

Data status. official KrisFlyer on Singapore Airlines, from Singapore: Saver rates per route, updated 28 Oct 2026 (Mainly Miles, sourced from SIA’s chart). chart Asia Miles on Cathay Pacific: distance bands effective 1 May 2026 (Award Travel Finder); the economy rate above 7,500 miles is my own extrapolation, and non-Hong Kong origins are priced on distance flown via Hong Kong. est. Everything else is a rough guess, because those programmes either price by revenue or I could not find a reliable chart. Use “Check on airline site” to read the real price, then type it into “Exact miles (k)” and it replaces the estimate and is remembered in this browser. Programme list and bank counts come from Suitesmile. Taxes, fees and the cost of reaching a nearby origin are not included.
